Seasonal Singalongs Uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Seasonal Singalongs Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Seasonal Singalongs channel to send the channel’s own videos to its own TikTok account.

What it uses

  • The sign-in token TikTok gives when the owner signs in, so the app can upload on the owner’s behalf.
  • Basic profile information of the owner’s account (display name, account ID), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The status of videos the app uploaded.

Where it is kept

Only on the owner’s own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok’s own.

What it does not do

It does not collect information about viewers or other TikTok users, does not sell or share any data, and does not use data for advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings, which ends its access; the local token is then deleted.

Contact

Through the Seasonal Singalongs channel on YouTube.
